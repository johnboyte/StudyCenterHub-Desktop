# StudyCenterHub Multi-Phase Operational Roadmap

The strategic vision and multi-phase roadmap for the StudyCenterHub platform across Desktop and Web environments.

---

## Strategic Vision

StudyCenterHub is the operational platform for the Real Life Study Center: a single system for hospitality, discipleship, study, community, volunteer coordination, communications, and daily operations.

---

## Phase Overview

### Phase 1 — Foundation & Core Systems (Completed)
- Baseline production deployment & dev/main branching model.
- Public QR self-registration and check-in system.
- Member Directory roster list & basic filtering.
- Administrative authentication & baseline deployment procedures.

### Phase 2 — Desktop Directory & Person Workspace (Active)
- Desktop local SQLite architecture & read-only presentation separation (`[PD-001]`, `[PD-002]`).
- Responsive split layout (55%/45%) and roster selection emphasis (`[PD-003]`).
- Person Workspace foundation across 5 standard sections (`[PD-004]`).
- Configurable Application Data principle & Note Types integration (`[PD-005]`).

### Phase 3 — Communications & Outreach Hub
- SMS, Email, and Voice integration.
- Automated follow-up queues, check-in alerts, and call rotation.
- Communication history timeline within Person Workspace.

### Phase 4 — Rich Constituent Context & Pathways
- Expanded constituent profiles (Photos, Emergency Contacts, School, Church, Mentors).
- Active pathway tracking, milestones, and participation progress.

### Phase 5 — AI Ministry Assistant
- Pattern detection (absence alerts, pathway completion, outreach recommendations).
- Automated daily briefing generation and operations summaries.

### Phase 6 — Study Center On-Site Operations
- Room scheduling, hospitality tracking, equipment checkout, and volunteer coordination.

### Phase 7 — Advanced Scheduling & Coverage
- Drag-and-drop shift scheduling, shift swaps, automated reminders, and coverage alerts.

### Phase 8 — Reports & Leadership Analytics
- Trend analysis, check-in analytics, visitor retention reporting, and executive metrics.

### Phase 9 — Production Hardening
- Automated backup verification, health monitoring, audit logging, and security hardening.

### Phase 10 — Enterprise Integrations
- Centralized sync server, public API integrations, and multi-location architecture.
